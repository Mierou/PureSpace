PureSpace

Cebu's community cleaning marketplace, as an interactive mobile demo with two dashboards that talk to each other.

  • Household: log in, post a request, accept a bid, chat, release payment
  • Clean & Earn: pick it on the login screen to land on the cleaner dashboard, bid on leads, complete the job, cash out
  • Log out from either profile to come back to the login. Progress is saved in this browser.
