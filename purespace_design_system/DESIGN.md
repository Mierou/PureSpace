## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a pristine, trustworthy, and community-centric aesthetic tailored for on-demand domestic cleaning marketplaces. The visual identity reflects hotel-grade cleanliness, reliability, and human warmth. It combines surgical clarity with accessible domestic comfort, making booking, tracking, and paying local independent service professionals effortless.

### Emotional Demeanor
- **Pristine & Orderly:** Crisp white surfaces and deep oceanic teal tones project hospital-grade sanitization and domestic security.
- **Approachable & Community-Rooted:** Generous touch targets, rounded geometries, and luminous golden validation accents create a friendly, dependable neighborhood atmosphere.
- **Transparent & Capable:** High-contrast data hierarchy for rates (in ₱ Philippine Peso), ratings, and verified credentials ensures unambiguous mutual trust between households and cleaners.

### Design Movement
Modern Corporate Clean with Tactile Fluidity: Soft ambient oceanic backdrops, crisp card containers with tailored corner radii, water droplet and sparkle micro-accents, and clear spatial hierarchy optimized for modern mobile-first experiences.

## Colors

The palette balances deep naval teal authority with clean water-inspired cyan accents and golden validation highlights.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy Dark (`#0F2634`):** Anchors high-priority structural elements, primary display typography, key headers, and core action surfaces.
- **Deep Marine Teal (`#00517C`):** Used for primary interactive triggers, active tab states, and key navigational landmarks.
- **Hydro Cyan (`#17A7E8`):** Conveys freshness, real-time booking progress indicators, active map tracking beacons, and interactive toggles.
- **Luminous Sun Gold (`#FEA327`):** Reserved for star ratings, verified community badges, celebratory confirmations, and special seasonal offerings.
- **Surface Spectrum:**
  - Base Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary cards and raised modals.
  - Off-Canvas Surface: Crisp Frost (`#F4F8FA`) for primary screen backdrops.
  - Inset Canvas: Pale Cerulean Tint (`#EBF3F7`) for segmented controls, badge backdrops, and inner metric blocks.
- **Text & Contrast Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Content: `#0F2634` (Navy Dark, high-contrast readability on light grounds).
  - Secondary Demarcation: `#516F84` (Muted Slate for sub-labels, timestamps, and secondary specifications).
  - Tertiary Footnotes: `#92A9B9` (Subtle tint for placeholder text, disabled states, and hairline dividers).
- **Feedback & Semantics:**
  - Success: `#10B981` (Completed job confirmations, payment receipt checkmarks).
  - Star Rating: `#FEA327` (Worker evaluation indicators).
  - Local Payment Integrations: Native brand accents for GCash and Maya are maintained cleanly within their respective transaction badges.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate balance between modern architectural geometric confidence and human-scale legibility.

- **Display & Headlines (`Outfit`):** Geometric, balanced, and fresh. The open letterforms and round geometry communicate purity, modern living, and friendly authority without corporate coldness.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Manrope`):** Engineered for dense mobile rendering. Manrope's open apertures, even stroke weights, and precise numeric glyphs provide effortless legibility for Philippine Peso rates (`₱`), multi-stage scheduling, and service checklists.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use tabular figures for pricing cards, hourly countdown timers, and booking totals.
- Currency symbols (`₱`) share the same weight and size as their corresponding numeric figures.
- Use `label-sm` with full uppercase tracking exclusively for contextual category tags, payment status chips, and verification pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for single-hand mobile ergonomics (optimized for 390px to 412px viewports) on modern Android devices, scaling gracefully up to tablet displays.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Base Grid:** 4-column layout on mobile devices (`< 600px`) expanding to 8 columns on tablet devices (`600px - 1024px`).
- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed `16px` (`margin`) outer edge gutters on mobile ensure maximum usable area while preventing edge-clipping on curved smartphone displays.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 8px base increment cadence. Stacked card groups, cleaning service packages, and profile detail screens utilize `space-md` (16px) inter-item spacing.
- **Thumb Zones:** Primary interactive actions (floating booking bars, next-step triggers) are anchored strictly within the lower 35% viewport zone with minimum tap clearances of `48px` to guarantee frictionless touch precision.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface-container contrasts and ambient, water-tinted drop shadows rather than heavy structural borders.

### Depth Stratification
- **Ground Floor (Elevation 0):** Pure screen foundation tinted in Crisp Frost (`#F4F8FA`).
- **Resting Level (Elevation 1):** Main service cards, cleaner profile listings, and interactive tiles rendered in `#FFFFFF`. Shadow style: `0px 2px 8px rgba(15, 38, 52, 0.04)`.
- **Raised Interactive (Elevation 2):** Floating search sheets, active booking cards, and bottom sheet containers. Shadow style: `0px 8px 24px rgba(15, 38, 52, 0.08)`.
- **Overlay & Navigation (Elevation 3):** Bottom navigation dock and primary floating action buttons. Shadow style: `0px 12px 32px rgba(15, 38, 52, 0.12)`.

### Border Philosophy
Elevated cards use a hairline boundary (`1px solid #EBF3F7`) to maintain crisp boundaries without dark lines, giving each component a clean, sanitized edge.

## Shapes

The design system employs Level 2 (Rounded) curvature across its interface library, striking an intentional balance between modern structural geometry and soft organic fluidity inspired by water droplets and clean surfaces.

### Radius Specifications
- **Standard UI Elements (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Checkboxes, small badge tags, numeric quantity pickers, and inline notification banners.
- **Large Components (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / `16px`):** Input fields, service selection tiles, modal dialogs, and segmented tab switchers.
- **Extra-Large Focal Cards (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / `24px`):** Cleaner profile cards, booking summary panels, promotional hero banners, and persistent bottom-sheet headers.
- **Pill Geometry:** Reserved strictly for interactive status chips, primary category pills, and full-bleed action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Marine Teal (`#00517C`) solid fill with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-xl`, height of `52px` (minimum `48px` tap target). Subtle press feedback: scale down to 0.98 with background darkening to Navy Dark (`#0F2634`).
- **Secondary Action:** Pale Cerulean (`#EBF3F7`) background with Deep Marine Teal (`#00517C`) text.
- **Accent Booking Trigger:** Star Rating Gold (`#FEA327`) fill with Navy Dark text for immediate checkout or instant quote validation.

### Cards
- **Cleaner Profile Card:** Pure White surface, `16px` padding, `24px` corner radius. Features cleaner avatar (with verified droplet sparkle badge overlay), name, verified service counts, star rating (`#FEA327`), distance indicator (e.g., "Mambaling, Cebu City • 1.2 km"), and starting hourly rate in tabular format (`₱250/hr`).
- **Package Selection Card:** Outlined in `1.5px solid #EBF3F7`. When selected, transitions to a `2px solid #00517C` border with an interior `#F4F8FA` background tint and top-right check badge.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** `52px` height, `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #EBF3F7` resting border, `rounded-lg` (16px), interior padding `16px`. Active focus state changes border to `#00517C` with zero shadow jump.
- **Address & Barangay Selector:** Includes a map pin icon (`#17A7E8`), bold barangay/street title, and muted secondary detail (`#516F84`).

### Chips & Filters
- Compact `36px` pills with `space-sm` horizontal padding. Resting: `#FFFFFF` with `#EBF3F7` border and `#516F84` text. Selected: `#00517C` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Accommodates icon prefixes (e.g., Deep Clean, Disinfection, Move-in/Move-out).

### Payment Integration Badges
- Standardized inline payment chips for local payment channels (GCash, Maya, Cash on Delivery). Features branded logo containers flanked by explicit total calculation strings in `₱` (Philippine Peso).

### Ratings & Feedback Display
- Compact inline badge: `#FEA327` star glyph paired with bold numerical rating (`4.9`) and total verified neighborhood reviews count (`(128)`) set in `#516F84`.
